# TalonInsight Refund Policy

**Last Updated: March 3, 2026**

TalonInsight (hereinafter "Service") is committed to ensuring customer satisfaction. The following refund policy applies to "Credits" sold through the Service.

### 1. Eligibility for Refund (Within 7 Days, Unused Credits)

Members may request a refund within **7 days** of the credit purchase date, provided that **all** of the following conditions are met:

* **Not a single credit** from the purchased credit package has been used.
* The daily complimentary "3 free analyses" do not affect refund eligibility; however, if even one paid credit has been deducted, a refund is not available.

### 2. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are not available in the following cases due to the nature of digital content:

* More than 7 days have passed since the purchase date.
* Any portion of the purchased credits has been used (the digital goods are considered partially consumed).
* Remaining credits at the time of voluntary account deletion.
* Credits issued free of charge through events, promotions, or other complimentary means.

### 3. Credit Protection for Analysis Failures

If an analysis fails to complete successfully due to a **technical fault on the part of the Service** (e.g., system error, server outage), credits will not be deducted for that session.

* If credits were deducted due to a service-side error, the system will automatically detect and restore the deducted amount, or the restoration will be processed manually following confirmation through customer support.
* Dissatisfaction with results caused by poor user internet connectivity or incorrect input is not considered an analysis failure and does not qualify for credit restoration.

### 4. Refund Process and Merchant of Record (MoR)

TalonInsight processes payments through global payment processors (Paddle, PayPal), and all refunds are handled in accordance with those platforms' procedures.

* **How to Request a Refund**: Email `taloninsight@gmail.com` with the email address used at purchase and your order number.
* **Processing Time**: Once a refund is approved by the operator, the actual credit to your account will be processed by Paddle or PayPal within approximately 3–7 business days, subject to their policies.
* **Fees and Exchange Rates**: Any withholding fees or currency exchange differences arising from the refund under the payment processor's policies may be borne by the Member.

### 5. Compensation Upon Service Discontinuation

In the event that the Service is permanently discontinued due to unavoidable circumstances, the operator will provide at least 30 days' advance notice and offer reasonable compensation for any remaining paid credits. (The scope of compensation will take into account factors such as the unlimited nature of the credits and the length of time the Member has already used the Service.)
