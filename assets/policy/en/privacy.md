# TalonInsight Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 3, 2026**

"TalonInsight" (hereinafter "Service") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users (hereinafter "Members"). We comply with applicable data protection laws and international regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This Privacy Policy explains how the Service collects, uses, and protects Member data.

### 1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

The Service collects only the minimum personal information necessary to provide its functionality.

* **Upon Registration**: Email address, name (or display name), and unique identifiers provided by social login providers (e.g., Google OAuth).
* **During Service Use**: Access logs, cookies, IP address, browser information, and usage history.
* **For Payments**: All payments are processed by external payment processors (Paddle, PayPal). **TalonInsight does not directly collect or store credit card numbers or bank account details.**

### 2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

* **Service Delivery**: Member identification, credit management, and delivery of app analysis results.
* **Payment Processing**: Verification of credit purchases and data reconciliation with payment processors (Paddle, PayPal).
* **Customer Support**: Responding to inquiries and delivering service notices.
* **Service Improvement**: Analyzing usage statistics to enhance features and strengthen security.

### 3. Third-Party Disclosure and International Data Transfers

The Service does not share Member information with third parties without consent, except as required for global payment processing. The following disclosures may involve international data transfers:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared | Retention Period |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Paddle.com** | Payment processing (MoR), tax calculation, fraud prevention | Email, name, payment method information | Until service termination or as required by applicable law |
| **PayPal** | Payment processing and settlement | Email, payment identifier | Until service termination or as required by applicable law |

### 4. Data Retention

* Member personal data is deleted promptly upon account withdrawal.
* For **dormant accounts (inactive for 2 years or more)** as defined in the Terms of Service, data may be deleted or stored separately following prior notice.
* Where required by applicable law (e.g., e-commerce regulations), data will be retained for the period specified by such law.

### 5. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Members may exercise the following rights regarding their personal data at any time:

* Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data.
* Request suspension of data processing or withdrawal of consent (account deletion).
* **GDPR Rights**: EU residents may exercise the right to data portability and the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").
* Rights may be exercised through the settings menu within the Service or by contacting us at `taloninsight@gmail.com`.

### 6. Cookies

* The Service uses cookies to optimize the browsing experience.
* Members may disable cookies through browser settings; however, certain features of the Service may be limited as a result.

### 7. Data Security

The Service takes the following measures to protect Member data:

* **Encryption**: All data in transit is encrypted using SSL/TLS.
* **Access Control**: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel, and regular security audits are conducted.
* **Security Practices**: Up-to-date security patches and authentication mechanisms are applied to protect against unauthorized access.

### 8. Data Protection Officer and Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries arising from use of the Service, please contact us at:

* **Email**: `taloninsight@gmail.com`
