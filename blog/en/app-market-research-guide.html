Why Is App Market Research Necessary?

Many solo developers believe "a great idea is all you need to succeed." But reality tells a different story. There are already millions of apps on the app stores, and users are already using apps that solve similar problems.

Developing an app without market research leads to these problems:

  • Entering an oversaturated market and losing to competition
  • Wasting time on features users don't want
  • Failing to monetize due to wrong revenue model choices
  • Poor marketing ROI

"Investing one week in market research can save you one month in development."

5 Steps to Free App Market Research

1 Define Your Target Category

First, clearly define which category your app belongs to. Refer to the category classifications on Play Store and App Store.

  • Main category (e.g., Productivity, Health & Fitness)
  • Specific use case (e.g., habit tracker, calorie counter)
  • Target users (e.g., professionals, students, parents)

2 Create a Competitor App List

Research the top 20 apps in your category. Select them based on these criteria:

  • Top-ranked apps in the category
  • New apps launched within the last 6 months
  • Apps solving similar problems
  • Apps with many reviews (validated demand)

3 Analyze Store Listings

Collect the following information from each app's store page:

  • App description: What value does it emphasize?
  • Screenshots: What are the key features?
  • Rating and review count: How satisfied are users?
  • Update frequency: Is it being actively developed?
  • Revenue model: Free? In-app purchases? Subscription?

4 Find Opportunities Through Review Analysis

Focus on analyzing negative reviews (1-2 stars) of competitor apps. This is where you'll discover unmet user needs.

  • "I wish it had~" = Feature request
  • "~doesn't work" = Bug or technical limitation
  • "~is inconvenient" = UX improvement opportunity
  • "~is too expensive" = Pricing strategy opportunity

5 Define Your Differentiation Points

Based on collected data, define what makes your app unique:

  • What problems haven't competitors solved?
  • Where can you provide a better user experience?
  • Is there a niche you can exploit with different pricing?
  • Can you focus more on a specific target audience?
